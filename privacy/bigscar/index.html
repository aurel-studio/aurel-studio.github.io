3. Ads, purchases and platform services

3-1. Rewarded ads: Google AdMob (Android and iOS)

  • When: On Android and iOS, until you buy the full game, the Google Mobile Ads SDK starts when the game launches and may preload an ad so it is ready. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one on the unlock screen, from floor 21 on. Watching one ad to the end opens the next 10 floors for that climb. There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads during combat. After you buy the full game, the ad SDK is no longer started from the next launch.
  • Who collects: Google LLC (USA), directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app. We do not receive or store this data; we only see aggregated AdMob reports.
  • Data: device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking) and app set ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, completed views, clicks), and crash and diagnostic data (errors, performance).

  • Consent: In the EEA and the UK (and Switzerland), a consent message from Google may be shown before ads are requested; there you can change your choice at any time with "Ad Privacy Options" in the game's Settings. On iOS, the IDFA is not used unless you allow tracking.
  • Managing your advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalized ads in your device settings. Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and do not allow it. Buying the full game stops the ad SDK from starting.
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy . Retention is governed by Google.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. Where the law requires consent, such as in the EEA and the UK, personalized ads are shown only with your consent.
