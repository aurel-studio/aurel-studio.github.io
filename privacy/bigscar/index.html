3-1. Rewarded ads: Google AdMob (free Android and iOS versions)

  • When: In the free version, the ad SDK starts when the game launches and may preload an ad so it is ready. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one on the unlock screen, from floor 21 on. There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads during combat. After you buy the full game, the ad SDK is not started.
  • Who collects: Google LLC (USA), directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app. We do not receive or store this data; we only see aggregated AdMob reports.
  • Data: device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking) and app set ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, ad interactions (impressions, completed views, clicks), and diagnostics (errors, performance).
  • Purposes: serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and, if you consent, personalized ads.
  • Consent: In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting ads. On iOS, if you do not allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, the IDFA is not used. Versions with ads will include an ad privacy option in Settings so you can change your choice at any time.
  • How to opt out: Buying the full game stops the ad SDK from starting. You can refuse personalized ads in the consent form or in the ad privacy option in Settings. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and do not allow it.
  • Retention: governed by Google. Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google processes this data as an independent controller. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are requested only after you make a choice in the consent form, and personalized ads only with your consent.

  • Payment methods and payment details are handled by Google and Apple. We never receive them.
  • The game receives a purchase confirmation from the store (product ID "full_game", purchase state, transaction token) and records "full game unlocked" on your device. It is not sent to any server of ours.
  • Refunds and purchase history are managed by Google Play or the App Store.

3-3. Achievements and leaderboards: Google Play Games, Apple Game Center, Steam

  • Only if you are signed in to that platform, the game sends the achievements you unlock and your records (such as the floor reached and time left) to that platform.
  • Leaderboards may show your platform nickname and score to other players.
  • Each platform's privacy policy applies. Purchases and play on the Steam version are handled by Valve through Steam.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: deleted when you uninstall the game. To delete it without uninstalling: on Android, open your device's app settings → Big Scars Remain → Storage → Clear data (menu names vary by device); on iOS, delete the app; on Windows, delete the folder %APPDATA%\BigScar. Climb Cards saved on PC are in Pictures\BigScar.

  • Ad data: delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings; if you have a Google Account, you can also use Google's My Ad Center.
  • Platform data: delete data held by Google Play Games, Game Center or Steam through that platform's settings or support.
  • Requests to us: thirtyshots2689@outlook.com. We hold no personal data, so where needed we will explain how to make the request to each platform.
