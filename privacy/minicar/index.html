2-3. 「의견 보내기」(이메일)

  • 게임 안의 「의견 보내기」를 누르면 이용자의 이메일 앱이 열리고, 받는 사람(개발자 주소)과 제목, 본문이 미리 채워집니다. 본문에는 문제를 찾는 데 필요한 앱 버전, 기기 모델, 운영체제 버전(Android 또는 iOS), 게임 진행 요약(주인공, 이야기 진행, 레벨)이 들어갑니다.
  • 이용자는 보내기 전에 내용을 확인하고 고치거나 지울 수 있습니다. 이용자가 이메일 앱에서 직접 보내기를 눌러야만 전송되며, 게임이 몰래 보내는 것은 없습니다. 이메일 앱을 쓰지 않고 위 주소로 직접 메일을 보내도 됩니다.
  • 메일을 보내면 개발자는 보낸 사람의 이메일 주소(이메일 앱이 함께 보내는 이름 포함)와 메일 내용, 이용자가 남겨 둔 위 정보를 받습니다.
  • 이 정보는 답장을 보내고 게임의 문제를 고치는 데에만 씁니다. 광고나 홍보에 쓰지 않으며, 다른 사람에게 제공하거나 판매하지 않습니다.
  • 답장과 문제 해결에 필요한 동안만 보관하며, 이용자가 삭제를 요청하면 지체 없이 지웁니다.
  • 메일은 개발자의 이메일 서비스(Microsoft Outlook.com) 계정으로 받으므로 Microsoft의 서버에 보관됩니다. 이 서버는 국외에 있을 수 있습니다.

2-4. TestFlight(iPhone 시험판)

iPhone 판은 Apple의 TestFlight 앱으로 나눠 드립니다. TestFlight는 Apple의 서비스로, 이용자가 TestFlight에서 직접 보낸 의견(스크린샷·글)과 앱이 멈췄을 때의 충돌 보고, 기기 모델·iOS 버전 같은 정보를 Apple의 방침에 따라 개발자에게 전달할 수 있습니다. 참가 방식에 따라 Apple이 시험 참가자의 이름이나 이메일 주소를 개발자에게 보여 줄 수 있습니다. 개발자는 이 정보를 시험 운영과 문제 해결에만 쓰고, 다른 곳에 쓰거나 제공하지 않습니다. 시험 참가를 그만두려면 TestFlight 앱에서 테스트를 중단하거나 앱을 지우면 됩니다.

앱이 요청하는 권한은 진동 하나입니다. 득템 연출, 결승선 통과, 코스아웃 같은 순간에 기기를 짧게 진동시키는 데 쓰며, 게임 설정에서 끌 수 있습니다. 인터넷, 위치, 카메라, 마이크, 연락처, 사진·파일 권한은 요청하지 않습니다. iPhone 판은 따로 권한을 요청하지 않습니다(진동은 권한 없이 동작합니다).

4. 하지 않는 것

1. Scope

This policy covers the offline beta of the game Minicar Champion Road: open testing on Android and TestFlight testing on iPhone. This version works without an internet connection.

2. What we handle

2-1. What we do not collect

We do not collect or store any personal data through the game, including your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. We run no servers for this game, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. The game sends no information to us.

2-2. Game data stored on your device

DataWhereWhy
Settings (sound, display, vibration, text speed, camera and so on)The app's private storage on your deviceTo keep your settings
Save slots (chosen hero, story progress, level, parts, machines and paint, in-game currency, attendance, missions and achievements, play time and so on)The app's private storage on your deviceTo continue and show records

The game reads your device's date and time for daily missions, attendance, offline auto racing and timed events, but never sends it off the device.

This data is never sent to us. The Android version uses Android's automatic backup. If you have backup turned on in your device settings, Google stores this data encrypted in your Google Account backup and restores it when you reinstall the game or move to a new device. That backup follows Google's policies and we cannot access it. If you turn backup off in your device settings, nothing is backed up. On iPhone, game data may be included in your iOS device backup (iCloud or computer backup); that backup follows Apple's policies and we cannot access it.

2-3. "Send feedback" (email)

  • When you tap "Send feedback" in the game, your email app opens with our address, a subject and a message already filled in. The message includes what helps us find problems: the app version, your device model, the operating system version (Android or iOS) and a short progress summary (hero, story progress, level).
  • You can read, edit or delete any of it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send in your email app, and the game never sends anything by itself. You can also write to the address above directly.
  • If you send the email, we receive your email address (including any name your email app sends with it), your message and whatever of the information above you left in.
  • We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems in the game. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we never share or sell it.
  • We keep it only as long as needed to reply and fix the problem, and we delete it without delay if you ask.
  • We receive email through our email service (Microsoft Outlook.com), so messages are stored on Microsoft's servers, which may be located outside Korea.

2-4. TestFlight (iPhone test version)

The iPhone version is distributed through Apple's TestFlight app. TestFlight is Apple's service: under Apple's policies it may pass on to us feedback you send through TestFlight yourself (screenshots and comments), crash reports when the app stops, and information such as your device model and iOS version. Depending on how you joined, Apple may show us a tester's name or email address. We use this only to run the test and fix problems, and we never use it for anything else or share it. To stop testing, stop the test in the TestFlight app or delete the app.

3. Permissions

The app requests one permission: vibration. It makes the device vibrate briefly at moments such as loot reveals, crossing the finish line or flying off the course, and you can turn it off in the game's settings. The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo and file permissions. The iPhone version requests no permissions at all (vibration needs none).

4. What we never do

Accounts or sign-up, servers of our own, ads, in-app purchases, analytics or crash-reporting tools, location, contacts, microphone or camera, chat, user-generated content, selling personal data. None of these are in this beta.

5. The online version and upcoming changes

An online version with accounts, cloud saves and server tournaments against real people will come later. Because it will process account and game records on a server, we will update this policy before it launches to explain what is processed and why, and ask for your consent where required. Game records from the beta will be reset in the online version.

6. Children's privacy

This beta collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Players under 14 should ask a parent or guardian for help before using "Send feedback". If a child under 14 has emailed us, we will delete the message without delay at a parent's or guardian's request.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: deleted when you uninstall the game (backup copies follow Google's retention on Android and Apple's on iPhone). To delete it without uninstalling, use Reset on the game's save-slot screen, or open your device's app settings → Minicar Champion Road → Storage → Clear data (menu names vary by device). On iPhone, deleting the app also deletes its data.
  • Emails you sent us: kept only as long as needed to reply and fix the problem, and deleted without delay if you ask at thirtyshots2689@outlook.com.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (the emails you sent), or to stop its processing. Write to the address above and we will act without delay. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have rights under the GDPR. We do not sell personal data.

9. Security

The game sends no personal data off your device. Emails you send stay in our email account and are not copied anywhere else.
