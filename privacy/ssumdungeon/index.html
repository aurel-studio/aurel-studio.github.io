1. Scope

This policy covers Aurel Studio's mobile game Ssum Dungeon (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.aurelstudio.ssumdungeon).

2. What we handle

2-1. What we do not collect

We do not collect or store any personal data, including your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos. We run no servers, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

3. Rewarded ads: Google AdMob (Android and iOS)

  • When: an ad plays only when you tap a "watch an ad" button on the result or preparation screen. There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads during combat. The Google Mobile Ads SDK starts when the game launches and may preload an ad so it is ready.
  • Who collects: Google LLC (USA), directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) in the app. We do not receive or store this data; we only see aggregated AdMob reports.
  • Data: device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking) and app set ID, device and app information (device model, operating system, app version), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, completed views, clicks), and crash and diagnostic data.
  • Purposes: serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and keeping ads secure, and, if you consent, personalized ads.
  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's UMP consent message is shown on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy settings" in the pause menu. On iOS you can choose "Ask App Not to Track" in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; the game and rewarded ads still work.
  • Under 14: for players who say they are under 14, we request only non-personalized ads (tagged as under the age of consent, max ad rating G).
  • Managing your advertising ID: Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy. Retention is governed by Google. Google processes this data as an independent controller.

4. What we never do

Accounts or sign-up, our own servers, analytics of our own, precise (GPS) location, contacts, microphone or camera, chat, user-generated content, in-app purchases, selling personal data, banner or interstitial ads. None of these are in the game.

5. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and players who say they are under 14 never receive personalized ads. If you believe a child's data was collected through the ad SDK, contact thirtyshots2689@outlook.com.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Ssum Dungeon → Storage → Clear data.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or use My Ad Center in your Google Account.
  • Requests to us: thirtyshots2689@outlook.com. We hold no personal data, so where needed we will explain how to make a request to Google.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal data, and withdraw consent. Users in the EEA and the UK have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to a supervisory authority. We do not sell personal data.

8. Security

We store no personal data. Files on your device are protected by the operating system's app sandbox, and the ad SDK communicates over encrypted connections as described by Google.
