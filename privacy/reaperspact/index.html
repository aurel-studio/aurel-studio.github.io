  • There is no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

2. Data stored on your device

Save files, settings (volume, text speed, etc.) and a daily counter of rewarded-ad uses are stored in the app's private storage. They never leave your device, are deleted when you uninstall the app, and we cannot access them.

4. Your choices

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • Opting out of personalised ads does not affect rewarded ads or their rewards.

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are limited to content rated T (Teen) or lower.

6. Retention

We do not keep any personal data. On-device data is deleted on uninstall. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies.
