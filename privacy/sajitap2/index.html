  • 목적: 광고 제공과 측정, 광고 사기 방지와 보안.
  • 동의: 유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 이용자에게는 Google의 동의 메시지가 표시될 수 있습니다.
  • 광고 ID 재설정·거부: 기기 설정 → 개인정보 보호(또는 Google) → 광고에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다.

1. Scope

This policy covers the game Sajitap 2: The Hand That Gives Back (Android, Windows).

2. What we handle

We do not collect or store any personal data. We run no servers, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. Whether you bought the full game is recorded only in the app's private storage on your device; it is never sent to us and is not included in Android automatic backup.

3. Ads and purchases

3-1. Rewarded ads: Google AdMob (Android)

  • When: until you buy the full game, the Google Mobile Ads SDK starts when the game launches and may preload an ad. An ad plays only when you tap "watch an ad" on the unlock screen shown when you climb past floor 3. Watching one to the end opens the next floor for that run. There are no banner or interstitial ads and no ads during combat.
  • Who collects: Google LLC (USA), directly through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We only see aggregated AdMob reports.
  • Data: device identifiers such as the advertising ID, device and app information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, completed views, clicks) and diagnostics.
  • Purposes: serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention and security.
  • Consent: users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may see Google's consent message.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, https://policies.google.com/privacy

3-2. Purchases: Google Play Billing (Android)

  • The only in-app product is the full game (product ID "full_game", one-time purchase). Floors 1 to 3 are free; the full game opens every floor without ads.
  • Google processes your payment method and details; we never receive them. The app only receives the purchase confirmation and records the unlock on your device.
  • Refunds and purchase history are managed in Google Play.

4. What we do not do

No accounts, no servers, no analytics of our own, no precise location, contacts, microphone or camera, no chat or user-generated content, and we never sell personal data.

5. Children

The game contains sword fighting and limb severing and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea).

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings and manage Google ad data in Google's My Ad Center. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing; residents of the EEA have rights under the GDPR. Contact: thirtyshots2689@outlook.com.
